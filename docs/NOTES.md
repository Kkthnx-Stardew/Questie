# What the journal notes say

This is the long form of the notes Questie adds to a quest in the journal. The README has the short version. Everything here is read from the game's own data, so what other mods add is covered the same way, unless a line says otherwise. The limits that go with it are in the README.

## Layout

Every note is a bullet in the same dash and space the game uses for a quest's rewards. A bullet that wraps continues under its text, a thin rule separates the game's own text from the notes, and there are no blank lines between notes, since the journal holds only about ten lines at a time. Several facts that belong together share one line. What to plant and how long it takes is one line, the hours and the weather a fish bites in are one line, and going back to someone and where they are now is one line.

## What each kind of quest gets

- The villager's portrait or the item's own picture in front of each name, so who and what is easy to see at a glance.
- How many you carry of what a delivery or gathering quest needs, with how many more lie in your chests and fridge, such as "You carry 0 Stone, have 50 in chests, and need 25." The game already counts kills, catches, pickups and greetings in the quest's own objective line, so Questie does not say those twice.
- For a fishing quest, where the fish bites, which seasons it bites in, at what times of day and in what weather, and whether it can be caught right now, or that it is out of season. The seasons come from the rules of each place, the ones the game catches fish by, so a fish that only bites in winter says so and one that can be caught year round on Ginger Island is not called out of season. A crab pot fish says which water. This is read from the game's own fish and location data, so it also covers fish and places added by other mods.
- For a delivery or gathering quest, which shops sell the item and for how much, worked out the way the shop works it out, so an item with no fixed price shows its real one. Festival stalls are left out.
- For the story quests that ask you to bring something, such as the largemouth bass for Jodi's fish casserole or the chocolate cake for Haley's cake-walk, how many you carry, where to take it, how to catch or cook it and where it is sold.
- For The Pirate's Wife, which only says to find a keepsake, which keepsake you carry, who to give it to next, what that person trades back and where they are.
- For a plain story quest that names its items only in its objective text, such as the five fish of Ol' Pappy Elixir, where and in which seasons each fish bites, where the other items are sold, foraged or dropped, or how they are made. A quest that names one item gets the full help, and one that names several gets a short line for each. Simple plurals such as "void souls" are read as the item they name. Every named item is marked in your backpack.
- For the rest of the game's story quests, what their objective leaves out: the Solar Essence for the sand dragon, the Void Mayonnaise for the goblin, the 100 Hardwood for the Giant Stump, where the Skull Key is used, that Krobus is in the sewer, where A Winter Mystery's figure hides, and the small steps of the beach, museum, gift, Community Center and lumber pile quests.
- For a building quest, who builds it and what it costs. For the quests about reaching a level in the mines or the Skull Cavern, how deep you have been.
- For an item quest, how to make the item and whether you know the recipe. When you know it, each ingredient shows how many you carry, such as "Egg (2 of 1)". When you do not, it says how to learn it, such as at 3 hearts with Emily or at Foraging level 6.
- For a crop, which seed to plant, the seasons it grows in, how many days it takes and whether it regrows, such as "Plant Parsnip Seeds in spring. It is ready in 4 days." This is read from the game's own crop data, so crops added by other mods are covered too.
- For an item that can be foraged, which places it can be found in and the seasons that apply at each, such as "Forage it in Backwoods in spring and Cindersap Forest in summer." This is read from the game's own location data, so forage added by other mods is covered too. A forage entry that names its item with an item query is expanded to every item the query can produce, so each of them gets the same places and seasons.
- For an item that monsters drop, which monsters drop it, where each can be found and how often, such as "Dropped by Frost Bat (95.5% in The Mines) and Bat (94% in The Mines and Skull Cavern)." The chances come from the game's own monster data, plus a small built-in table for the extra drops the game adds in monster code, such as the Ectoplasm of a Ghost during the Wizard's special order, the Prismatic Jelly of a Prismatic Slime, the Red Cabbage Seeds of a Serpent or Mummy, the Dinosaur Egg and bones of a Pepper Rex, and the seeds and tubers of a Tiger Slime. Monsters added by other mods are covered by the data; their code-added extras are not. The places are the game's own fixed spawn places; a monster added by another mod shows its chance without a place unless it reuses the id or name of a known monster.
- Who is left to meet in a meeting quest, and where a person is right now (or that they are here).
- Where a lost item lies, until you find it.
- For a delivery of a fish, such as Fish Stew's albacore, Wanted: Lobster or a fish an expansion adds, the same fishing help a fishing quest gets. Places are cut to three, with the base game's places first and a count of the rest.
- For a kill quest, where the monster lives, until the kills are done. A quest for several kinds at once, such as any slime, names no place.
- For an item a machine makes out of another item, which machine and what goes in, such as "Made with the Keg from Hops." for pale ale. A machine that takes several things names them all, such as Milk or Large Milk for cheese, and at most two machines are named. This is read from the game's own machine data. A machine that makes whatever goes in, such as wine from any fruit, names no single item and says nothing.
- A shop that asks more than ten times what the cheapest one does is left off the list, and a shop from another mod that has no named owner is left off too, since its id is not a name a player knows.
- A special order's journal page names only the objectives you carry items for, or says you carry nothing for them yet, since the game lists every objective just below. Its notes use the same bullets and rule as a quest page.
